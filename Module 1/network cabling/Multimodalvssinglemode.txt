Single-mode fiber (SMF)
- Core: ~8-10 µm (cladding 125 µm)
- Light path: One mode/path, no modal dispersion
- Light source: Laser (1310 nm / 1550 nm)
- Distance: Long - 10 km to 100+ km
- Bandwidth: Very high
- Attenuation: Low
- Cost: Fiber cheap, optics/transceivers costly
- Use: Telecom, WAN, FTTH, undersea cables, campus backbone
- Jacket color: Often yellow

Multimode fiber (MMF)
- Core: 50 µm or 62.5 µm (cladding 125 µm)
- Light path: Many modes/paths, modal dispersion
- Light source: LED / VCSEL (850 nm / 1300 nm)
- Distance: Short - typically up to ~300-600 m
- Bandwidth: Lower than SMF
- Attenuation: Higher
- Cost: Cheaper optics/connectors, easier to align
- Use: Data centers, LAN, inside buildings, short links
- Jacket color: Orange (OM1/OM2), aqua (OM3/OM4), lime green (OM5)

Simple rule:
Short distance + cheaper = multimode.
Long distance + high bandwidth = single-mode.
